# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as contributors and maintainers of the Earth Endowment Observatory pledge to make participation in this project a respectful, evidence-grounded, and harm-aware experience for everyone — regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

EEO works in domains — natural resources, labor, indigenous rights, ecological harm, public revenue — where power is unequal and exposure can cause real harm. We expect conduct in this project to reflect the same care for human dignity and safety that the project applies to its evidence work.

## Our standards

**Expected behavior:**

- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Respect different perspectives, experience levels, and disciplinary backgrounds.
- Accept constructive criticism gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project's public-interest mission.
- Show care for people who may be affected by the evidence this project handles.
- Distinguish your views from the project's institutional positions.

**Unacceptable behavior:**

- Harassment, intimidation, or threats of any kind.
- Discriminatory jokes, slurs, or derogatory language.
- Publishing private information about any person without their consent (doxxing).
- Using the project's public platform to make unauthorized factual claims about individuals, communities, or organizations.
- Attempting to use this repository or its issue tracker to expose sensitive evidence, personal data, or safety-critical information that belongs in governed internal channels.
- Behavior that would be professionally or personally harmful in a public-interest research environment.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in all project spaces — the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other forum managed by this project. It also applies when a person is representing this project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainer at Admin@clubout.club.

All complaints will be reviewed confidentially. The project team is obligated to maintain confidentiality about the reporter. If the maintainer is the subject of a complaint, report to a trusted third party familiar with the project.

The project team may take any action deemed appropriate, including a warning, temporary ban, or permanent exclusion from the project.
