# Data Policy

## Scope

This repository contains a corridor prototype for public-interest evidence workflows. It uses synthetic/sample data for public surfaces unless explicitly marked otherwise.

## Data handling rules

- Do not commit secrets, credentials, private legal notes, or raw restricted evidence.
- Do not commit precise sensitive geospatial coordinates.
- Do not ingest or expose personally identifying sensitive reports.
- Treat all correction and review notes as internal workspace data.

## Publication posture

- Publish only evidence that has passed method and exposure review.
- Keep disclosure tiering explicit (open, contextual, aggregated, restricted, suppressed).
- Preserve correction pathways and right-of-reply pathways in every release.
