# Disclosure Policy

## Principle

Universal knowledge does not require universal exposure.

## Mandatory controls

- Use tiered disclosure for claims, evidence, and map layers.
- Do not publish unverified allegations about identifiable persons.
- Do not publish sensitive community or sacred-site information.
- Do not publish exact vulnerable ecological coordinates.
- Do not imply legal findings without authoritative legal/regulatory basis.

## Product constraints

- No global atlas for MVP.
- No composite score/rankings/certification layer.
- No blockchain trust claims.
- No AI authority over legal, confidence, or publication decisions.
