# AI Use Policy

## Allowed use

AI assistance may be used for:

- code drafting and refactoring,
- documentation drafting,
- summarization of non-sensitive project artifacts,
- implementation planning.

## Not allowed

AI must not:

- make final legal conclusions,
- override review gates,
- assign publication confidence without reviewer accountability,
- expose restricted evidence or sensitive map detail.

## Human accountability

Final decisions for review status, disclosure tier, release approval, and correction adjudication remain with designated human reviewers.
