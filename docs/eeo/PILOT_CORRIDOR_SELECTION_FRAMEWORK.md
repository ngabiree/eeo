# EEO Pilot Corridor Selection Framework

## Purpose

The first EEO pilot must prove the observatory pattern without becoming a global resource registry. Selection should narrow to one commodity, one geography, and one complete endowment-to-economy chain that can be studied with public-interest value, source discipline, governance humility, and exposure safeguards.

## Selection Doctrine

The pilot should remain:

- universal in analytical concern, but narrow in release scope;
- plural in governance, with rights-holder and jurisdictional authority respected;
- careful in disclosure, especially where labor, community, ecological, ownership, or geospatial exposure could create harm;
- humble in authority, making no legal finding unless clearly quoting a competent authority;
- fierce in public truth, refusing vague public-benefit claims, traceability overclaims, certification theater, or hidden value-capture assumptions.

## Minimum Pilot Shape

A candidate pilot is eligible only if it can be represented as:

1. one commodity;
2. one geography;
3. one endowment-to-economy chain;
4. one evidence dossier;
5. one public-safe corridor dashboard;
6. one release manifest;
7. one correction and right-of-reply path.

## Positive Selection Criteria

Prefer a candidate corridor when:

- the public-interest question is clear and material;
- endowment, governance, ownership, labor, trade, ecology, public revenue, and value-capture questions can be connected without making unsupported claims;
- public or consent-cleared source material is available;
- relevant legal, governance, and rights-holder contexts can be named at safe granularity;
- the corridor can be described without exact sensitive geospatial disclosure;
- right-of-reply routes can be identified for high-impact actor-specific claims;
- the pilot can demonstrate EEO's reasoning layer without ranking countries, companies, or communities.

## Disqualifiers

Hold or reject a candidate when:

- publication would require exact sensitive site coordinates;
- indigenous, local, or rights-holder governance obligations are unknown or cannot be respected;
- the strongest available sources are allegations without safe right-of-reply handling;
- the work would imply legal findings EEO is not competent to make;
- the pilot would become a global atlas, registry, ranking, certification, or investment signal;
- trade data would be the main basis for physical chain-of-custody claims;
- public revenue data would be treated as proof of durable public benefit;
- labor, community, ecological, or ownership exposure risks cannot be mitigated through aggregation, verified access, community-governed handling, or suppression.

## Required Pre-Publication Gates

Before a real pilot can move beyond internal review, the release package must include:

- source inventory and license/use-basis review;
- evidence-claim metadata for every public claim;
- legal-posture labels and jurisdiction-specific review for sensitive claims;
- indigenous data governance and rights-holder review where applicable;
- sovereignty and public-authority context review;
- exposure ethics review for labor, community, ecological, ownership, and geospatial risks;
- right-of-reply status for high-impact actor-specific claims;
- disclosure-tier decision for each claim and dataset;
- signed release manifest;
- correction and maintenance path.

## Output Of Selection

The selection process should produce a short internal selection memo with:

- recommended commodity and geography;
- public-interest rationale;
- proposed endowment-to-economy chain boundary;
- source availability summary;
- known governance and rights-holder considerations;
- exposure-risk register;
- initial disclosure-tier map;
- validation plan;
- explicit hold conditions.

## Non-Goals

The pilot selection process must not produce:

- real-world public allegations;
- rankings or scores;
- certification claims;
- investment recommendations;
- exact sensitive geospatial downloads;
- global coverage commitments;
- community reporting intake;
- claims of physical traceability from trade data alone.
