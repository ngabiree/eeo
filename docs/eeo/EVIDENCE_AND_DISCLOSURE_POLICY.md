# Evidence and Disclosure Policy

## Required Claim Metadata

Every public claim must include:

- claim ID;
- claim text;
- claim type;
- confidence label;
- evidence layer;
- granularity class;
- legal posture;
- disclosure tier;
- source title;
- source locator;
- source date;
- retrieval date;
- license or use basis;
- method or derivation;
- confidence explanation;
- stale-after date;
- review status;
- correction path;
- right-of-reply status.

## Claim Types

Observed, official, modeled, inferred, alleged, disputed, confidential, withdrawn.

## Confidence Labels

Verified, official, modeled, estimated, self-reported, third-party reported, community-reported, disputed, restricted, outdated, unknown.

## Evidence Layers

Factual, analytical, normative, legal.

## Legal Posture

No legal claim, public-record description, allegation by source, finding by authority, disputed legal status, requires legal review.

## Disclosure Tiers

| Tier | Label | Handling |
|---:|---|---|
| 0 | Open | Low foreseeable harm; lawful; clear public-interest value |
| 1 | Contextual public | Public with caveat, uncertainty, method note, or right-of-reply status |
| 2 | Aggregated | Publish at safer geography/entity aggregation |
| 3 | Verified access | Limited to approved users |
| 4 | Community-governed | Subject to rights-holder authority |
| 5 | Suppressed | Do not publish |

## Unsafe Claim Patterns

Rewrite or suppress claims that:

- convert allegations into findings;
- call a risk indicator proof;
- call customs/trade data traceability;
- call public revenue public benefit;
- call spatial proximity causation;
- imply consent without authority;
- infer beneficial ownership as fact without source-backed review;
- use community or worker reports without protection protocols.

## Safe Language

Use:

- "public records state...";
- "the cited source reports...";
- "available evidence suggests...";
- "this estimate is derived from...";
- "this raises a public-benefit question...";
- "EEO makes no legal finding...";
- "the evidence is insufficient to determine...";
- "reported trade data does not prove physical chain-of-custody."
