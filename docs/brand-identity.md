# EEO Brand Identity Doctrine

EEO’s brand is **quiet authority**: an observatory, record office, and civic evidence chamber.

It should feel precise, warm, restrained, and sovereignty-aware — never accusatory, gamified, extractive, sensational, or surveillance-like.

## Core posture

- Evidence-first.
- Limitation-forward.
- Dossier-first.
- Non-adjudicatory.
- Source-humble.
- Correction-ready.
- Rights-aware.
- Sovereignty-respecting.

## Public language rules

Public surfaces should emphasize evidence, limitations, correction pathways, source humility, review status, and public-safe summaries.

Avoid language that implies:

- adjudicated guilt;
- universal truth;
- real-time tracking;
- certification;
- ranking;
- scoring;
- surveillance;
- final authority over states, communities, firms, workers, ecosystems, or resources.

## Visual identity

The visual system should evoke:

- institutional trust;
- public record;
- earth systems;
- civic stewardship;
- restrained luminosity;
- careful review.

The Imana Star may serve as symbolic doctrine: wisdom, will, and way — but never as a claim of ownership, spiritual authority, state authority, or cultural extraction.

## Design principle

Do not make EEO look like a dashboard chasing attention.

Make it feel like a chamber where evidence is handled carefully.
