# ADR-002: No Universal Atlas in MVP

**Status:** Accepted  
**Date:** 2026-04-26

---

## Context

A "global atlas of natural endowments" is an appealing vision. It is also a serious harm surface: it can expose sacred sites, vulnerable communities, artisanal mining settlements, endangered species locations, and sensitive infrastructure at global scale if insufficiently governed.

A global atlas also implies that EEO is a replacement for national statistical systems, indigenous institutions, SEEA, EITI, and other authoritative sources — which it is not and should not claim to be.

## Decision

EEO does not build or launch a universal atlas in the MVP. The pilot is scoped to one corridor.

Route `/atlas` is explicitly deferred. It must not appear in the published application until a separate governance decision has been made with appropriate safeguards review, rights holder consultation, and legal review.

## Consequences

- The product launches as a corridor evidence dossier, not an atlas.
- Geospatial content in the MVP is limited to the pilot corridor and subject to map-safety review (see ADR-005 and `docs/map-safety-protocol.md`).
- EEO's identity as a complement to, not replacement of, authoritative systems is preserved.
- Expansion to additional corridors is permitted under the same discipline, not as a blanket atlas rollout.
