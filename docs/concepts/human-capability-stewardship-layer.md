# Human Capability, Labor, Stewardship, and Relationship Layer

## Doctrine

Human beings are not Earth endowments. Human beings are rights-bearing,
knowledge-bearing, laboring, creative, governing, ecological, cultural, and
intergenerational agents within endowment systems.

Earth's endowments become economic value through human capability: labor,
knowledge, technology, care, law, institutions, imagination, and ecological
relationship. But human beings are not resources to be inventoried. They are
agents whose dignity limits what may be extracted, whose labor and knowledge
make value possible, whose communities may govern and steward endowments, whose
vulnerability may absorb hidden costs, and whose future capability is the
ultimate test of whether endowment use is legitimate.

## Live evidence principle

EEO should allow live data work, verified reporting, restricted evidence
handling, and raw source preservation inside governed systems. What must not be
live by default is uncontrolled public exposure.

Core rule:

```text
EEO should be live where evidence is governed, and restrained where evidence is exposed.
```

## Public repository boundary

This public repository may contain:

- application code;
- public documentation;
- schema and migration scaffolding;
- public methods;
- synthetic sample data;
- non-sensitive issue and product discussion.

This public repository must not contain:

- raw evidence;
- restricted files;
- sensitive geospatial data;
- partner-confidential data;
- personal data;
- production database dumps;
- legal review notes;
- secrets;
- right-of-reply packets;
- sensitive community, worker, or human-capability data.

## Core test

A corridor is not successful because it exports more. It is successful only if
it strengthens people, respects rights, repairs ecological damage, distributes
benefit, and leaves future generations with more capability rather than less.

## Red lines

- No classification of human beings as natural resources.
- No individual talent scores.
- No public labor-availability maps that enable exploitation.
- No race, ethnicity, caste, tribe, religion, disability, or migration-status proxy for talent.
- No publication of sensitive community knowledge without authority and consent.
- No public mapping of vulnerable groups at actionable resolution.
- No use of capability data for coercive recruitment, surveillance, policing, union avoidance, displacement, or targeting.
- No reduction of human flourishing to wages, GDP, productivity, or fiscal revenue.
