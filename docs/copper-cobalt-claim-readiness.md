# Copper-Cobalt Claim Readiness Matrix

Last updated: 2026-07-29

This matrix reviews the current Copper-Cobalt corridor claims against EEO release discipline. It is an internal readiness aid, not a public finding and not a signed release manifest.

EEO publishes evidence for inquiry, not a verdict. A claim should not move into a release manifest until source rights, evidence links, source limitations, review posture, disclosure safety, correction path, and right-of-reply posture are coherent for that claim type.

## Current release posture

- Current release-scoped claim: `CLAIM-DRC-CO-001`
- Current release posture: one low-exposure methodological claim is release-scoped.
- Other visible dossier claims should remain withheld from release scope until claim-specific review is complete.
- Withheld status is publication discipline. It is not a finding that a claim is false, disputed, unlawful, or unsafe in all forms.

## Readiness statuses

- `release-scoped`: already appropriate for the current manifest scope.
- `awaiting accountable review`: source, wording, and internal evidence structure are prepared, but required human review and release authority are not complete.
- `near-candidate`: likely next candidate after source/license and review confirmation.
- `needs method review`: evidence and interpretation limits need reviewer sign-off.
- `needs exposure review`: public harm, rights, sensitivity, or map-safety review blocks release.
- `needs right-of-reply review`: actor-affecting posture must be assessed before release.
- `defer`: keep out of release until stronger governance, evidence, or safeguards exist.

## Stop conditions before adding any withheld claim to a release manifest

Do not add a withheld claim to the public release manifest if any of the following remain unresolved:

- source license or lawful-use basis;
- source date, accessed date, or stale-after posture;
- claim method and confidence explanation;
- legal posture;
- disclosure tier;
- map-safety or sensitive-location risk;
- right-of-reply need for materially affected identifiable actors;
- correction and withdrawal path;
- reviewer or accountable role;
- reviewer authorization and dated decision;
- conditions attached to an approval;
- public-safe explanation of what the evidence does not prove;
- exact correspondence between the approved claim version and the manifest version.
