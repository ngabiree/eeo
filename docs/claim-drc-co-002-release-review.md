# CLAIM-DRC-CO-002 Release Readiness Review

Last updated: 2026-07-29

## Purpose

This document records a bounded release-readiness review for `CLAIM-DRC-CO-002`, the descriptive claim that the Democratic Republic of the Congo holds the largest identified cobalt reserves of any single country.

This review is an internal publication-discipline record. It is not a public finding, independent geological verification, legal determination, certification, or authorization to expand the current release manifest.

## Verdict

**Conditional candidate; not ready for manifest inclusion.**

The claim is structurally stronger than the other currently withheld claims because it is descriptive, low-exposure, linked to an official source, assigned high confidence, marked approved for release in the claim record, and does not require right of reply in its current aggregate form.

It should nevertheless remain withheld until its source provenance and release authorization are citation-grade and explicit.

## Current strengths

- Claim type: descriptive.
- Legal posture: factual observation.
- Exposure risk: low.
- Confidence: high.
- Review status in the claim record: approved for release.
- Right-of-reply status: not required for the current aggregate wording.
- Evidence link: `EVID-USGS-CO-002`.
- Source link: `SRC-USGS-CO-001`.
- The evidence record identifies methodological revision, host-country reporting, and site-level interpretation limits.
- The claim states what it does not prove and what evidence would revise it.
- The claim does not identify a mine, operator, concession, community, household, or sensitive location.

These strengths support continued review. They do not by themselves authorize release.

## Must fix before manifest inclusion

### 1. Citation-grade source locator

The current source record points to a general USGS National Minerals Information Center page rather than the exact 2026 *Mineral Commodity Summaries: Cobalt* document and relevant table, page, or section.

Before release, record:

- exact document title;
- edition or version;
- publication date;
- stable document URL or archival locator;
- relevant page, table, or section;
- accessed date;
- replacement or update relationship to earlier editions.

A general institutional landing page is insufficient for a material public claim.

### 2. Explicit lawful-use basis

The current source record labels the license status as `open`, but does not explain the precise reuse basis.

Before release, record:

- public-domain, license, terms-of-use, or other lawful-use basis;
- attribution requirement;
- whether EEO is quoting, paraphrasing, extracting data, or reproducing a table;
- any redistribution or modification limits;
- the accountable source steward who confirmed the posture.

Public accessibility must not be treated as automatic permission for unrestricted reuse.

### 3. Exact wording verification

A reviewer must confirm that the phrases `largest identified cobalt reserves` and `substantial majority of global known reserves` accurately reflect the cited edition and denominator.

The review must distinguish, where relevant:

- reserves from resources;
- identified or reported reserves from independently verified geological quantities;
- country estimates from global totals;
- current estimates from durable geological fact;
- economic recoverability from physical occurrence.

If the exact proportion is not presented or is unstable, use narrower language such as:

> The cited USGS edition reports that the Democratic Republic of the Congo has the largest cobalt reserve estimate among individual countries.

### 4. Review and authorization record

Before inclusion, record:

- accountable evidence steward;
- method reviewer;
- review date;
- confidence explanation;
- disclosure tier;
- stale-after date;
- correction and withdrawal path;
- explicit release decision;
- exact release-manifest version.

A claim-level `approved_for_release` field does not substitute for a signed or otherwise accountable manifest decision.

## Disclosure, map safety, and right of reply

For the current national-level, descriptive wording:

- proposed disclosure posture: contextual public;
- geographic granularity: national;
- map-safety risk: low, provided no exact mine, concession, community, or vulnerable location is added;
- right of reply: not required in the present aggregate form;
- legal posture: no legal claim.

Reassess these conclusions if the claim is expanded to identify companies, concessions, sites, rights-holders, disputed reserve ownership, extraction conditions, or national performance.

## Public-safe interpretation boundary

The claim may support a limited statement about the scale and geographic concentration of reported cobalt reserves.

It does not establish:

- physical chain of custody;
- current production volume;
- economically recoverable quantity under all price and technology conditions;
- ownership or entitlement;
- extraction legality;
- environmental or labor conditions;
- public benefit;
- future supply security;
- EEO independent verification of the underlying reserve estimate.

## Release decision

Keep `CLAIM-DRC-CO-002` in `withheldClaimIds` until all must-fix conditions are complete.

After completion, the release authority may:

1. include the claim in a new manifest version with its exact source and limitations;
2. retain it as withheld if provenance or wording remains inadequate;
3. narrow or revise the claim;
4. defer it pending a newer authoritative edition.

This review does not authorize option 1 automatically.
