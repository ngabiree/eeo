# Right-of-Reply Protocol (Prototype)

## Purpose

Provide a structured path for named parties to challenge factual framing, interpretation limits, or potential harm from publication.

## Workflow

1. Intake request from correction route or reviewer channel.
2. Link request to affected claim(s).
3. Record review activity and status transitions.
4. Apply revision, maintain challenge note, or withhold claim if unresolved.

## Constraints

- No adjudicatory language.
- No implication of legal guilt.
- Maintain evidence and method boundaries in all responses.
