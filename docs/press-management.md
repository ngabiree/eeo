# Press Management Protocol (v0.9)

EEO press practice is part of publication governance, not a separate marketing layer.

## Core rules

- EEO press materials must not say more than the evidence supports.
- Every press release or public announcement should include a plain-language limitation note and correction route.
- Press materials must not convert risk indicators into legal conclusions.
- No press material should be released before the relevant dossier, claim, or report has passed its release gate.

## Press release rules

- Reference only published dossier/claim/evidence outputs.
- Include explicit limitations in plain language.
- Include correction route and right-of-reply framing where relevant.

## Spokesperson rules

- Spokespersons must stay within release-manifest scope.
- Avoid adjudicatory language unless backed by authoritative legal/regulatory findings.

## Embargo rules (if used)

- Embargo drafts must preserve the same evidence boundary as public release.
- No additional speculative interpretation in embargo-only text.

## Limitation and evidence boundary rules

- State what evidence does not prove.
- State uncertainty and source constraints.
- Do not imply causation, liability, or product-level traceability from insufficient evidence.

## Correction and update rules

- Corrections should be logged through the established correction route.
- Press corrections should reference updated claim/release-manifest state.

## Contested-claim handling

- Use right-of-reply discipline for materially affected identifiable actors.
- Do not frame dispute status as legal adjudication.

## Company/government response handling

- Receive and review submissions through governance workflow.
- Update public text only after evidence/review gate completion.

## Community, worker, and ecological safety handling

- Avoid publishing details that increase risk to communities, workers, or sensitive ecosystems.
- Respect access-governance and map-safety decisions.

## Emergency correction / takedown

- If harmful overexposure occurs, restrict/take down affected material, log incident response, and publish correction/update once reviewed.

## Anticipated misreadings and safe corrections

- Misreading: "This proves product-level traceability."  
  Safe correction: This dossier does not establish product-level traceability. It explains what public production and trade data can and cannot prove.

- Misreading: "This proves company liability."  
  Safe correction: This material does not adjudicate legal liability. It presents evidence-linked observations with limitations.

- Misreading: "This ranks countries or firms."  
  Safe correction: EEO does not publish scoring or ranking systems in this prototype.

- Misreading: "This means all resource data should be public."  
  Safe correction: Universal analytical concern does not require universal exposure; some data must be aggregated, restricted, or not collected.

- Misreading: "This is a forecast."  
  Safe correction: Current outputs are evidence-linked observations and claims, not forecasts.

- Misreading: "This is a legal finding."  
  Safe correction: EEO publication is not a legal adjudication unless citing authoritative legal/regulatory findings.

- Misreading: "This identifies wrongdoing."  
  Safe correction: Public text should avoid wrongdoing conclusions without authoritative legal/regulatory basis.
