# Language Safety Checklist

Use this manual checklist for public copy, dossier summaries, and claim text.

## High-risk terms requiring authoritative support

- guilty
- liable
- illegal
- corrupt
- corruption proven
- violation established
- caused harm
- owns this resource
- proves
- traceable to this mine
- official finding
- convicted
- responsible for harm

These terms are not permanently banned, but they require authoritative legal, regulatory, or evidentiary support and publication review approval.

## Preferred safer framing

- public data indicate
- reported
- inferred
- not established
- requires stronger evidence
- does not prove
- under review
- challenged
- restricted
- withdrawn
- evidence suggests
- this source contextualizes
- this source limits
- this claim does not establish

## Rule

EEO should not use adjudicatory or accusatory language unless supported by authoritative legal or regulatory findings and approved through publication review.

Press copy must be reviewed for overclaiming because media summaries can amplify weaker language into stronger public conclusions.
