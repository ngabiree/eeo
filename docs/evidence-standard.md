# Evidence Standard (Prototype)

## Claim requirements

Each public claim should state:

- what is claimed,
- what evidence supports/limits/contradicts,
- confidence and legal posture,
- what it does not prove,
- what would revise it,
- review status and correction path.

## Evidence role discipline

Evidence is not automatically proof. Roles include support, limit, contradiction, contextualization, and review motivation.

## Publication posture

Publish only what has passed method and exposure checks, with explicit caveats and known limitations.
