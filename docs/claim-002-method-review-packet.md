## Review purpose

This packet organizes the evidence and release questions for the claim that the Democratic Republic of the Congo is the leading cobalt reserve jurisdiction and held about 55% of estimated global cobalt reserves in the cited 2026 USGS material.

It is an internal review aid. It is not an EEO geological finding, legal determination, ownership statement, certification, or release approval.

## Proposed bounded wording

> The U.S. Geological Survey's 2026 cobalt materials identify the Democratic Republic of the Congo as the leading cobalt reserve jurisdiction and estimate that it held about 55% of global cobalt reserves.
