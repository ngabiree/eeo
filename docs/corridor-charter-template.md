# Corridor Charter Template

Use this template before authorizing any EEO corridor beyond the current Copper-Cobalt pilot.

A corridor charter is an authorization record, not a public finding. It determines whether a proposed corridor is sufficiently bounded, sourced, reviewable, and safe to move from roadmap or candidate status into active internal work.

EEO publishes evidence for inquiry, not a verdict.

## Corridor status

Assign exactly one status.

- `roadmap`: named as a possible future direction only.
- `candidate`: early scoping and source discovery are allowed; no public claim surface.
- `provisional`: bounded enough for internal dossier scaffolding; still not authorized for public claims.
- `authorized`: charter requirements are satisfied and named reviewers have approved active internal construction.
- `active_internal`: evidence, claims, review, and release artifacts are being prepared internally.
- `limited_beta`: public-safe review with selected partners or reviewers.
- `public`: released through a signed manifest and correction route.
- `paused`: stop condition or dependency blocks work.
- `retired`: no longer maintained as an EEO corridor.

Do not infer status from public interest, commodity importance, preliminary research, or visual readiness.

## 9. Stop Conditions

Pause or keep the corridor internal if any condition applies.

- Source license or lawful-use basis is unresolved.
- Evidence provenance is missing.
- Material claims lack review.
- Corridor boundaries remain ambiguous.
- Severe evidence gaps would make the public narrative misleading.
- Rights-holder authority or required consent is unresolved.
- Exact geography creates foreseeable harm.
- Correction path does not exist.
- Required right-of-reply has not occurred.
- Legal review identifies unacceptable exposure.
- Disputed evidence is presented as settled fact.
- A source has become stale for its intended use.
- Release manifest is incomplete.
- Responsible reviewers cannot approve release.
